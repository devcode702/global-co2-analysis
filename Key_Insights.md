# 🌟 Key Insights from CO₂ Emissions Analysis

1. **Global Trend**:  
   CO₂ emissions have steadily increased since the industrial revolution, with notable acceleration after the 1950s.

2. **Top Emitters**:  
   China, USA, and India are consistently the highest contributors.

3. **Per Capita Emissions**:  
   Developed countries (USA, EU nations) show higher per capita emissions compared to developing countries.

4. **Continental Trends**:  
   Asia is the fastest-growing contributor, while Europe shows a slight decline due to policies on renewable energy.
